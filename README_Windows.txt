Nothing to install (no Python, no other software). Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

1) Unzip ENS_Tools_Windows.zip (right-click > Extract All).
   Do not start the programs from inside the zip.

2) Start:
   - ENS_Image_Database\ENS_Image_Database.exe   (project #5, image database)
   - Spectral_Analyzer\Spectral_Analyzer.exe     (project #6, spectral analysis)

3) On the first start of the image database, select:
   - the Excel workbook (the most recent version:
     Results Patho_DH definitif 06-10-2025 CLEMENT.xlsx)
   - the image folder (the per-slide .zip archives work directly)
   - (optional) the folder of spectral stacks

Good to know
- Each .exe must stay in its folder, next to its "_internal" folder.
- Keep the two folders ENS_Image_Database and Spectral_Analyzer side by side:
  the "Open in Spectral Analyzer" button of the image database needs it.
- If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC":
  click "More info", then "Run anyway"
  (the program is not signed; this is expected).
- The data (Excel workbook, images, spectra) are not in this zip:
  copy them separately and select them on the first start.
